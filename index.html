Emergencies

What to do first, before you call us.

Four situations where the first two minutes matter more than the phone call.

Gas leak

Leave the building immediately, do not use electrical switches. Call the National Gas Emergency line on 0800 111 999, then call us.

Electrical fault

Switch off the consumer unit if safe to do so. Do not touch any exposed wiring. Call us on 020 8478 9992 for emergency attendance.

Flood or major leak

Turn off the water at the stopcock (usually under the kitchen sink). Place towels or containers to limit damage, then call us immediately.

Fire

Get everyone out, close doors behind you, call 999. Never go back in. Once everyone is safe, call us to arrange emergency repair & temporary housing.

Common Questions

Switching Managing Agents FAQ

Can I switch agents while a tenant is in the property?
Yes. Switching managing agents does not require ending the tenancy or asking your tenants to leave. We coordinate a seamless management transfer in the background while your tenant stays settled in their home, introducing our team smoothly with updated rent payment details and emergency contacts.
What happens to the tenant deposit?
Tenant deposits remain protected under an authorised scheme (DPS, TDS, or MyDeposits). We coordinate the formal transfer of the deposit registration between agency accounts in full compliance with statutory rules. Exact transfer mechanics depend on whether the deposit is held in a custodial or insured scheme.
Will All Seasons contact my current agent?
Yes. Once you have agreed on a transition plan and served the required written notice under your existing agency terms, we handle direct liaison with the outgoing agency to collect keys, inventory documentation, compliance records, and tenancy files.
How long does the handover normally take?
The timeline primarily depends on the contractual notice period specified in your existing agency agreement (typically between 30 and 90 days). Once the notice period concludes, the practical physical key handover and digital file transfer is typically completed within 3 to 5 working days.
